# 💸 Money Path

A small, replayable finance game you play in your browser. Live out 24 months of financial life: **earn** an income, **budget** it against real-life surprises, resist (or give in to) **spending temptations**, and **invest** to watch your money compound — all while keeping yourself happy.

**▶️ Play it:** once GitHub Pages is on, visit `https://vendetta1777.github.io/money-path/`

## How it works

Each month folds three classic money lessons into one turn:

1. **Budget** — Your income arrives (sometimes with a random life event: a car repair, a bonus, a raise). Cover your *needs* first, then split what's left between **Fun**, an **Emergency Fund**, **safe investing** (bonds), and **risky investing** (stocks).
2. **Temptation** — A tempting purchase appears. Buy it for an instant happiness boost, or keep the money working for your future self.
3. **Market** — Your investments rise and fall based on a random news headline. Stocks swing hard; bonds drift up slowly.

## Why it stays fun

- **4 starting situations** (Student, Gig Worker, Young Professional, Single Parent) that play very differently.
- **Random events, temptations, and market news** every month — no two runs are alike.
- **Score = Net Worth × Happiness**, so hoarding every penny *or* blowing it all both lose. The skill is balance.
- Lose by **burning out** (happiness hits 0) or **drowning in debt** (cash flow, not net worth, sinks you first).

## The finance concepts it teaches

Budgeting · emergency funds · compound interest · opportunity cost · risk vs. reward · diversification · cash flow vs. net worth.

## Tech

A single self-contained `index.html` — plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step, no dependencies. Open the file or host it anywhere static.

## Run locally

Just open `index.html` in any browser. That's it.
